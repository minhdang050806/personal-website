RESEARCH

Geometry, dynamics, and trustworthy medical AI.

My long-term goal is to build learning systems that model structure and motion while remaining reliable when assumptions change between benchmark evaluation and real deployment.

01

3D / 4D Computer Vision

Geometry-aware representations for dynamic scenes, including mesh-based and implicit neural representations for structure and motion.

02

Medical Imaging

Cardiac imaging, segmentation, reconstruction, and clinically meaningful measurements from echocardiography and volumetric data.

03

Reliable Deployment

Failure monitoring, uncertainty, auxiliary-variable shift, cross-dataset generalization, and evaluation protocols that reflect deployable inference.
